\chapter*{Resumen}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Resumen}
\label{cap:resumen}

\textbf{Problema.} La mayor\'ia de cl\'inicas veterinarias, en Ecuador y
contextos similares, siguen operando con registros manuales o en papel,
lo que provoca p\'erdida de expedientes, dificultad para consultar el
historial cl\'inico y ausencia de indicadores de gesti\'on
b\'asicos~\cite{cedeno2021veterinaria}. \textbf{Soluci\'on.} BIOPET es un
sistema web de gesti\'on veterinaria (Spring~Boot/Java, Angular,
PostgreSQL, Redis/Valkey) con autenticaci\'on y autorizaci\'on por
rol/propiedad, CRUD de mascotas, m\'odulos de citas, consultas y vacunas,
y acceso JPA a rutinas almacenadas. \textbf{Metodolog\'ia.}
\emph{Engineering Research}/\emph{Design Science} (Peffers
et al.~\cite{peffers2007dsrm}) con marco
Goal--Question--Metric~\cite{vansolingen2002gqm}, sin dise\~no
experimental controlado.
\textbf{Principales validaciones.} 205 pruebas automatizadas (0 fallos, 0
errores); cobertura JaCoCo de 91{,}80\,\% en l\'ineas y 79{,}39\,\% en
ramas (umbral 70\,\%); rendimiento con k6 (0{,}0\,\% de error); usabilidad
SUS sobre 18 participantes (media 74{,}44/100, IC~95\,\%
$[63{,}33, 85{,}56]$); seguridad auditada (OWASP Top~10 manual; SpotBugs
est\'atico, 0 hallazgos \texttt{SQL\_*}, y auditor\'ia de SQL din\'amico
en rutinas almacenadas, 0 hallazgos; ZAP din\'amico: \emph{baseline}
hist\'orico y escaneo autenticado local con TLS, ambos sin alertas
altas); y despliegue real en producci\'on (Render, HTTPS activo).
Software, evidencias y backend quedan archivados con DOI real
(\texttt{\doisoftware}/\texttt{\doidataset}, Zenodo) y \emph{digest}
inmutable (GHCR). \textbf{Conclusi\'on principal.} BIOPET demuestra, con
evidencia reproducible en todas sus dimensiones de calidad
(ISO/IEC~25010~\cite{iso25010-2011}), que es posible construir y evaluar
emp\'iricamente, en un Proyecto Fin de Curso, un artefacto funcional,
seguro seg\'un los controles auditados y reproducible; las limitaciones
de alcance (entorno acad\'emico, tama\~no muestral) se documentan en el
cap\'itulo de amenazas a la validez.

\textbf{Palabras clave:} gesti\'on veterinaria; aplicaciones web;
Spring~Boot; Angular; JWT; PostgreSQL; ISO/IEC~25010; usabilidad (SUS);
seguridad OWASP; reproducibilidad.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\label{cap:abstract}

\textbf{Problem.} Most veterinary clinics, in Ecuador and similar
contexts, still rely on manual or paper-based records, causing lost
files, difficulty retrieving clinical history, and the absence of basic
management indicators~\cite{cedeno2021veterinaria}. \textbf{Solution.}
BIOPET is a web-based veterinary management system (Spring~Boot/Java,
Angular, PostgreSQL, Redis/Valkey) with role/ownership-based
authentication and authorization, pet CRUD, appointments, consultations
and vaccines modules, and JPA access to stored routines.
\textbf{Methodology.} Engineering Research/Design Science (Peffers et
al.~\cite{peffers2007dsrm}) with a Goal--Question--Metric
framework~\cite{vansolingen2002gqm}, tying claims to real evidence, with
no controlled experimental design. \textbf{Main validations.} 205
automated tests (0 failures, 0 errors); JaCoCo line coverage of
91.80\,\% and branch coverage of 79.39\,\% (70\,\% gate); k6 performance
testing (0.0\,\% error rate); usability measured with SUS on 18
participants (mean 74.44/100, 95\,\% CI $[63.33, 85.56]$); security
audited (manual OWASP Top~10; SpotBugs static analysis, 0
\texttt{SQL\_*} findings, plus a dynamic-SQL audit of the stored
routines, 0 findings; dynamic OWASP~ZAP: a historical baseline scan and
an authenticated passive scan on the local TLS environment, both with no
high-severity alerts); and a real production deployment (Render, HTTPS
enabled). Software, evidence and backend image remain archived with a
real DOI (\texttt{\doisoftware}/\texttt{\doidataset}, Zenodo) and an
immutable \emph{digest} (GHCR). \textbf{Main conclusion.} BIOPET shows,
with reproducible evidence across every quality dimension
(ISO/IEC~25010~\cite{iso25010-2011}), that it is possible to build and
empirically evaluate, within a capstone course project, a functional
artifact that is secure with respect to the audited controls and
reproducible; scope limitations (academic environment, sample size) are
documented in the threats-to-validity chapter.

\textbf{Keywords:} veterinary management; web applications;
Spring~Boot; Angular; JWT; PostgreSQL; ISO/IEC~25010; usability (SUS);
OWASP security; reproducibility.
